About Cannabox

Modern CBD vending solutions bringing quality wellness products to the heart of Budapest, available 24/7.

Our Mission

Cannabox makes premium CBD products accessible when and where you need them. Located in the heart of Budapest at Gozsdu Udvar, our smart vending machines offer 70+ quality CBD products 24/7. No waiting, no hassle – just fast, convenient access to oils, gummies, chocolates, and more with simple card or NFC payment.

Quality You Can Trust

We carefully select products from trusted European brands that meet strict quality standards. All items contain less than 0.2% THC and comply with Hungarian and EU regulations. From CBD oils and gummies to unique Tyson 2.0 products, every item is chosen for quality and safety.

Convenience Meets Innovation

Our vending machines use smart technology to keep products fresh and transactions secure. Located at 1072 Budapest, Dob u. 16., right at the entrance of Gozsdu Udvar, we're open non-stop with contactless payment options. Whether it's morning or midnight, Cannabox is there when you need it.
